# Azghan Readme

## Branch Usage Guidelines

This branch is dedicated to ongoing development for the AgriSmart mobile app. All team members must follow these instructions when working in this branch:

### Before Working
- **Notify the team**: Inform all members before starting work on this branch.
- **Pull latest changes**: Ensure your local branch is up-to-date.

### While Working
- **Commit regularly**: Make clear, descriptive commits.
- **Avoid conflicts**: Coordinate with others to prevent merge issues.

### After Working
- **Push your changes**: Upload your work to the main branch.
- **Notify the team**: Let everyone know when you have finished your updates.

---

Please follow these steps to maintain smooth collaboration and avoid conflicts.
